Our craft

One hook, one skein, one row at a time.

Hook & Hearth is a one-person studio. Every order is worked from a written pattern — chain, single crochet, double crochet, row after row — using DK and worsted-weight yarn sourced from small mills. Nothing is mass-produced, and nothing is rushed.

Each product page lists the actual hook-to-finish time and yarn weight, the way a pattern book would, so you know exactly what you're holding: not a factory item, but hours of someone's hands.
